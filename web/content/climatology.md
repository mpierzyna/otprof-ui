### → A climatology of optical turbulence over the Netherlands

Twenty years of ERA5 pressure level data[^sample] (2005-2025, 0.25°, 3-hourly) are processed with OTProf to obtain twenty years of $C_n^2$ profiles in the training domain.
For each profile, Fried parameter $r_0$ and scintillation index $\sigma_I^2$ are computed.
The profiles and integrated parameters are then aggregated in time as shown in the plots of this website.

At each location, we obtain:
- an aggregated[^stat] $C_n^2$ profile, $r_0$, and $\sigma_I^2$ per month.
- an overview of the annual cycle, i.e., all monthly aggregated[^stat] profiles and parameters.
- an overview of the aggregated[^stat] diurnal cycle for the selected month.

All views except the diurnal cycle can show an filtered aggregations covering a) all hours of the day, b) day-only (12UTC), or c) night-only (00UTC).

[^sample]: Currently <span data-clim-months>fewer</span> months: the ERA5 data are still being downloaded and processed.
[^stat]: median or statistic as selected.
