### Disclaimer: Experimental research product

The optical turbulence (OT) forecasts and climatology on this page are experimental research products generated by OTProf, a machine-learning model. They serve as technology demonstrators and are intended to give a qualitative, order-of-magnitude picture of the spatio-temporal evolution of OT ($C_n^2$) in the Netherlands.

**The OTProf products are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Use for operational decisions is entirely at your own risk; the authors and Technische Universiteit Delft accept no liability.** Validation against local observations is strongly encouraged. Forecasts may be delayed, incomplete, or discontinued without notice, and methods may change between versions.

**Known limitations**

- **Trained on model output, not observations.** OTProf learns from Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) model simulations and inherits WRF's known biases relative to observations. Fine-tuning on observations is future work.
- **No horizontal consistency.** OTProf is a 1D column model. Each grid column is predicted independently. Interpret spatial patterns with caution.
- **Different input data for forecasts.** The climatology is derived from ERA5, matching the setup evaluated in our [publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09346). The forecasts, however, use GFS input, which comes from a different weather model with its own errors and biases. We have not yet assessed performance on GFS input but expect it to be qualitatively similar.
- **Performance.** Model skill and limitations are documented in our [publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09346). Users are responsible for familiarizing themselves with them.
